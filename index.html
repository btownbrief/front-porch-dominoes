How to play

Play a tile that matches either open end. No match? Draw from the boneyard until you can play or it runs dry. Empty your hand to score every pip left around the porch.

A blocked round goes to the lowest pip count. First to 100 wins the match. Doubles sit crosswise, as tradition demands.
